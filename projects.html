Advanced Materials · 2025

Hydrogen-Bond-Engineered Analog Memory

Hydrogen bonding suppresses interfacial traps to stabilize analog conductance updates.

>8× lower trap density · 7 × 7 crossbar

View details
Research question
Can hydrogen-bonded interfaces reduce trap-driven variation in analog synaptic updates?
Physical mechanism & approach
PVA hydroxyl groups bond with surface iodides in CsPbI₃, reducing interfacial traps and stabilizing ion transport. Engineer the PVA–CsPbI₃ interface and compare analog conductance updates with device and array measurements.
Experimental evidence & results
DFT and electrical analysis; fabricated 7 × 7 crossbar; image-classification evaluation. >8× lower interfacial trap density · ≈12.5 pJ/update · >10⁴ s retention at 85 °C.
My contribution
First author of the published study.

Advanced Materials · 2026

Helicity-Defined Persistent Analog Memory

Optical helicity programs persistent conductance states that remain electrically readable in a device array.

72 distinguishable states · 10⁶ s retention

View details
Research question
Can optical helicity program durable analog states that remain electrically distinguishable in an addressed array?
Physical mechanism & approach
Chirality-matched circularly polarized light conditions persistent conductance updates in layered chiral perovskite synapses. Measure state separability and retention, then evaluate a PCB-integrated 7 × 7 device array with ADC/FPGA readout.
Experimental evidence & results
State-resolved retention and read-noise analysis; array conductance maps and mixed-signal vector–matrix multiplication. 72 statistically distinguishable states through 10⁶ s · 7 × 7 array · PCB-integrated readout.
My contribution
Co-first author; the publisher credits conceptualization, methodology, investigation, data curation, and original-draft writing.

Related publications

Advanced Functional Materials · 2025

Trap-Engineered Optoelectronic Memristors

An ALD TiO₂ interlayer reduces interface traps and enables low-power optoelectronic memory.

15× lower trap density · ≈0.7 µW switching power

View details
Research question
How can an intermediate layer improve low-power optoelectronic memory?
Physical mechanism & approach
An ALD TiO₂ nanofilm passivates perovskite-interface defects and improves electron-transport alignment. Compare trap density and switching behavior in devices with and without the intermediate layer.
Experimental evidence & results
Electrical and optical device characterization; 5 × 5 array image-recognition evaluation. 15× lower interfacial trap density · +0.24 V SET · ≈0.7 µW switching power.
My contribution
First author of the published study.

Chemical Engineering Journal · 2024

Molecular and ALD-Based Interface Engineering

Molecular contacts and ALD-grown oxide electrodes provide complementary interface strategies for photovoltaic and capacitor devices.

View details
Research question
How do molecular contacts and ALD-grown electrodes change charge extraction across device platforms?
Physical mechanism & approach
SAM dipoles tune electrode work function and hole extraction; a separate ALD study develops laminated In₂O₃:V₂O₅ electrode stacks. Study these interface strategies separately in organic photovoltaics and capacitor devices.
Experimental evidence & results
Published device studies of SAM-treated contacts and ALD-processed laminated electrodes. Two first-author papers connect interface design with photovoltaic and capacitor operation.
My contribution
First author on both published papers.

Journal of Flexible and Printed Electronics · 2026 (Accepted)

Printed Interconnects and Electrical Reliability

Printed-film thickness helps explain resistance variation between stretchable interconnect traces.

View details
Research question
How much of the electrical variation between nominally identical stretchable traces originates in printed-film thickness?
Physical mechanism & approach
Printed-film thickness contributes to initial trace-to-trace resistance variation; deformation-related changes are analyzed separately. Compare film-thickness differences with electrical characterization across printed liquid-metal-particle traces.
Experimental evidence & results
Accepted manuscript record on thickness and trace-to-trace electrical variability; related stretchable-PCB project. Distinguishes process-origin electrical variation from resistance changes during repeated deformation.
My contribution
First author on the accepted manuscript; participating researcher in the related industry project.
