Molecular control of artificial synapses

Can molecular interactions make artificial synapses more linear and symmetric?

My role
First author of the associated Advanced Materials paper.
Approach
Use hydrogen bonding as a molecular design variable to study linear and symmetric synaptic response.
Key outcome
Hydrogen-bond-driven control of artificial synapses connects molecular interactions with neuromorphic device behavior.

05 / Published work · 2023

ALD oxide electrode platforms

Can ALD-grown oxide electrodes serve both photovoltaics and capacitors?

My role
First author of the associated Journal of Alloys and Compounds paper.
Approach
Process laminated In₂O₃:V₂O₅ nanocompounds by ALD and examine their use as electrodes in different device architectures.
Key outcome
Laminated In₂O₃:V₂O₅ nanocompounds establish an electrode platform spanning organic photovoltaics and capacitor devices.
  • Atomic layer deposition
  • Laminated oxide films
  • Electrode integration

Related papers

  • Atomic layer deposition-processed laminated In2O3:V2O5 nanocompounds: Versatile electrode platforms for high-performance organic photovoltaics and capacitor deviceJournal of Alloys and Compounds · 2023
