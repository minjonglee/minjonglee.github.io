Core research

Chiral & optoelectronic memory

Research question

How can optical helicity be encoded as a persistent, distinguishable electronic memory state?

What I control
Optical helicity · electrical programming conditions
What I measure
Retained conductance · read-noise-limited state capacity · retention · array response
Why it matters
An optical input can become a persistent analog state rather than a transient polarization-sensitive response.

A co-first-author Advanced Materials study demonstrated 72 distinguishable conductance states retained for 10⁶ s. A separate read-noise analysis predicted a capacity of 72.3 levels; a 7×7 device array was evaluated for computing.

Research vision

From interface-controlled transport to reliable, integrated memory

Published device studies establish how interfaces and defects affect transport and analog states. My ongoing work tests limits to memory precision and reliability; integrated memory hardware remains a longer-term research direction.

  1. How do molecular interfaces and defects govern electronic and ionic transport?
  2. What limits the precision, stability, and reliability of emerging memory states?
  3. How can device-level physics guide scalable memory and computing hardware?
