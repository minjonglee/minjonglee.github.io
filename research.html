Research

From interface physics toward integrated electronic systems.

Interfaces, defects, and ionic dynamics shape charge transport and state formation. I study their consequences for device reliability and emerging memory, building on organic, hybrid, and flexible electronics.

Connected device platforms

Optoelectronics & hybrid devices

Organic photovoltaics, photodetectors, transparent electrodes, and charge-selective molecular interfaces are a foundation of my research. They provide a connected platform for studying how contacts and traps shape transport, with future possibilities in optoelectronic memory and sensory hardware.
