Research

From interface physics toward integrated electronic systems.

Interfaces, defects, and ionic dynamics shape charge transport and state formation. I study their consequences for device reliability and emerging memory, building on organic, hybrid, and flexible electronics.

Core science

Interfaces · Defects · Ions · Transport

Research question

How do molecular-scale interfaces and defects reshape electronic and ionic energy landscapes?

What I control
Molecular contacts · thin-film interfaces · defect environments
What I measure
Charge transport · trap behavior · state-formation dynamics
Why it matters
Interfacial physics links material chemistry to device behavior and reliability.
