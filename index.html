01

Established expertise + current research

Interfacial & Nonequilibrium Physics

How do molecular-scale interfaces and defects reshape electronic and ionic energy landscapes?

Molecular contacts and local defects set the conditions for transport. I connect interface engineering in optoelectronics with trap control and ionic dynamics in emerging memory.

Self-assembled monolayers · Thin-film interface engineering · Electrical characterization

Explore this pillar ↗
02

Established expertise + current research

Adaptive & Reliable Memory Devices

How can interfacial and defect physics be translated into reliable—or deliberately stochastic—memory behavior?

Memory depends on how a device changes state and how well it holds that state. My work links molecular and interfacial control to synaptic devices, optoelectronic memristors, and capacitor platforms.

I–V / C–V measurements · Pulse testing · Interface / electrode engineering

Explore this pillar ↗
03

Established foundations → emerging directions

Integrated Memory & Computing

How can emerging memory and low-temperature electronic devices be integrated into scalable computing hardware?

ALD oxide electrodes, thin-film processing, and device characterization form the experimental foundation. I aim to extend this foundation toward hardware-aware modeling and integrated memory–logic systems.

Atomic layer deposition · Thin-film device processing · I–V / C–V / pulse testing

Explore this pillar ↗

Connected research / Foundation

Organic & Hybrid Optoelectronics

Organic photovoltaics, photodetectors, transparent electrodes, and charge-selective molecular interfaces are a foundation of my research. They provide a connected platform for studying how contacts and traps shape transport, with future possibilities in optoelectronic memory and sensory hardware.
