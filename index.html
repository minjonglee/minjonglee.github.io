Research Work

Physical mechanisms demonstrated in functional hardware.

Advanced Materials · 2025 First author

Hydrogen-Bonded Analog Memory

PVA–CsPbI₃ hydrogen bonding suppresses interfacial traps and stabilizes linear, symmetric analog updates. A fabricated 7 × 7 crossbar and image-classification evaluation connect device physics to computation.

>8× lower interfacial trap density · ≈12.5 pJ/update · >10⁴ s at 85 °C

Advanced Materials · 2026 Co-first author

Helicity-Encoded Analog Memory

Optical helicity conditions persistent, electrically readable conductance states. A PCB-integrated 7 × 7 array with ADC/FPGA readout validates mixed-signal vector–matrix multiplication.

72 distinguishable states · 10⁶ s retention · PCB/ADC/FPGA readout

Research Outlook

From Understanding Devices to Engineering Reliable Systems.

I aim to connect device physics with reliable memory, computing hardware, and integrated electronic systems.

Predictive Memory Reliability

Can read noise and stochastic state evolution reveal the origins of analog memory variability and anticipate reliability loss?

Hardware-Aware Memory Computing

How do switching variation and read noise propagate into array errors, and which device or peripheral choices can reduce them?

Integrated & Adaptive Electronics

How do interface defects and processing conditions govern leakage and long-term stability in advanced thin-film and capacitor structures?
