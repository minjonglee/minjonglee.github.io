About me.

A researcher working between thin-film materials, electronic devices, and dependable systems.

01 / ABOUT

Research rooted in
device physics.

I am an integrated M.S.–Ph.D. researcher in the School of Electrical Engineering at Korea University, advised by Professor Jae Won Shim.

My work spans memristors for compute-in-memory and neuromorphic functions, DRAM MIM capacitors, and thin-film device integration. I use electrical characterization and physics-guided analysis to understand how interfaces, charge transport, and defects shape device performance and stability.
