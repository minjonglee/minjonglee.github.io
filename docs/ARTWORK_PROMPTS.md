# Scientific artwork generation prompts

Mode: built-in image generation (`stylized-concept`), one output per prompt. All eight were requested as high-resolution 8:5 landscape assets. The common negative instruction was: no text, labels, letters, numbers, scale bars, arrows, charts, fabricated measured data, logos, watermarks, neon, cyberpunk, or intense glow. The images are conceptual editorial artwork rather than scientific evidence.

1. **Featured chiral synapse:** Elegant scientific editorial visualization of a chiral perovskite optoelectronic synapse. Close oblique view of a realistic thin crystalline film within a refined device; restrained left- and right-handed circular polarization paths, chiral molecular/crystal ordering, and a quiet progression of analog synaptic states conveyed by material tone and microstructure rather than a chart. White warm-neutral setting, navy/muted blue, tiny crimson accent, soft studio light, generous negative space. Do not assert an unverified device stack.
2. **Featured hydrogen-bond synapse:** High-end scientific artwork of a layered thin-film artificial synapse, intimate cutaway. Make the molecular-scale interface and a delicate hydrogen-bond network between polymer and perovskite regions the focus, with sparse guided ionic motion and stable symmetric analog response suggested by material texture. White warm-neutral background, restrained navy, blue and crimson, natural studio light. Distinct from the chiral-light artwork and not a simple stack diagram.
3. **Featured optoelectronic memristor:** Realistic thin-film optoelectronic memristor at an oblique microscopic perspective. Soft incoming light, semiconductor layers, an extremely thin TiO₂-like intermediate interface passivating sparse traps, and orderly carrier/ion motion. Emphasize interface engineering and low-power switching without numerical claims. Warm-white backdrop, muted navy/blue and restrained crimson, elegant material detail.
4. **Research interfaces:** Sophisticated microscopic material landscape at the boundary of two thin-film materials. Sparse local defects/traps, ordered but nonuniform ions near the interface, and subtle charge-transport paths. Show local energy-landscape variation through texture rather than a graph. Warm-white studio setting, navy/muted blue and tiny crimson; independent composition from the Featured Work device images.
5. **Research memory:** Realistic layered emerging-memory material in close cutaway. Controlled ionic redistribution and several subtly distinct stable internal states suggested by material domains, conveying switching precision and reliability without plotted endurance data. Emphasize physical film and interfaces, not a generic chip; light neutral background, navy/blue with spare crimson ion accents.
6. **Research optoelectronics:** Refined thin-film optoelectronic material stack in oblique close-up, translucent upper layers receiving diffuse photons, fine charge-selective molecular interface, and restrained carrier extraction visible through subtle material/light effects. Express photovoltaic and photodetector research without stock solar panels. Warm-white light, navy and muted blue, fine crimson accents.
7. **Research flexible:** Realistic thin-film substrate with fine interconnects and device islands bending around a clean curved surface. Microstructural detail and gentle strain should express materials/device R&D and electrical continuity. Avoid a stock circuit board, wearable product, or hand. Warm-white studio setting, navy/blue, restrained metallic details.
8. **Research integration:** Clearly prospective physical-scale progression from one refined thin-film device in the foreground to a modest ordered device array and an abstract system substrate in the distance. Connect them subtly without implying a demonstrated integrated prototype. Generous white space, finely detailed materials, navy/blue with slight crimson, soft studio light.
